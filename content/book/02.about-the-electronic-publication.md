<h1>About the Electronic Publication</h1>

<p>Katherine MacNaughton’s book, <cite>The Development of the Theory and Practice of Education in New Brunswick 1784–1900</cite>, based on her 1945 MA thesis, has been re-issued in a new edition, an electronic book, as part of the University of New Brunswick's 225<sup>th</sup> anniversary celebrations in 2010. The project is a result of a partnership between the university’s Archives and Special Collections department, and the Electronic Text Centre at UNB.</p>
